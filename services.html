05 / PREVENTION MATTERS

Small Steps Can Make a Big Difference.

Professional pest management works best alongside good property maintenance and everyday prevention habits.

  • Store food in sealed containers and clean spills promptly.
  • Repair leaks and reduce unnecessary moisture.
  • Keep bins covered and remove waste regularly.
  • Seal suitable gaps around doors, pipes and windows.
  • Arrange an inspection when you notice signs of pest activity.
